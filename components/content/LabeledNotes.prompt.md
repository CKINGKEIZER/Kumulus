# LabeledNotes
Small-caps sub-headers each over a bullet list (cash-flow notes panel, strategic-vision outlook).

```jsx
<LabeledNotes boxed groups={[
  {label:"Net working capital", items:["NWC needs decrease over the period despite rising revenue","Going-forward cash impact expected to be limited"]},
  {label:"Capex", items:["Navision ERP, office, R&D, servers & licences — €54k-€124k"]},
  {label:"Operational free cash flow", items:["Growing, predictable EBITDA + low NWC + low capex → strong recurring cash"]},
]} />
```
