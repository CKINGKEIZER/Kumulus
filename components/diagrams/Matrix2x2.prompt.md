# Matrix2x2
Positioning map: two labelled axes + four quadrants + items plotted by (x,y) in 0..1. Our company passed with `highlight:true`.

```jsx
<Matrix2x2 xAxis={{label:"Product focus"}} yAxis={{label:"Geographic reach"}}
  items={[{x:0.8,y:0.75,label:"Company X",highlight:true},{x:0.3,y:0.4,label:"Competitor A"},{x:0.6,y:0.2,label:"Competitor B"}]} />
```
