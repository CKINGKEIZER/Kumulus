Session 3 — how Kumulus writes. Mined from 753 real bullets, 116 titles across the Bones & Torino IMs. Learn structure and tone; never copy deal-specific sentences into unrelated companies. See sell-side-writing-guide.md.

Sentence families

direct-declarative
Company/offering definition; opening a description.
[Company] is/operates [what it is] [where].
“BeMedico is an independent full-service distributor of medical devices in the Benelux, partnering with top global manufacturers and healthcare professionals.”
“Extensive portfolio of high-end single-use products such as implants, braces and injection materials, used across multiple specialties in daily care.”
attribute-plus-evidence
Assert a quality and immediately substantiate it.
[Attribute claim], [reflected in / evidenced by] [metric/fact].
“An average employee tenure of around 9 years reflects strong loyalty, low turnover and a cohesive internal culture.”
“High recurring level of sales as a result of the single-use nature of products.”
evidence-plus-implication
Lead with a fact, draw the investment implication.
[Fact/metric], [creating/entailing] [implication].
“Customer loyalty is improved through loan and consignment stock models that embed BeMedico into hospital workflows and entail high switching costs for healthcare professionals.”
“Consumables account for the largest share of revenue, creating a recurring and predictable sales base.”
cause-and-effect
Market condition -> opportunity.
[Condition] [creates/increases/favours] [effect].
“Market fragmentation and rising compliance costs create opportunities for roll-up strategies.”
“Growing regulatory complexity increases demand for specialized product knowledge and local compliance support.”
capability-plus-outcome
Operating capability -> commercial result.
[Capability] [guarantees/reinforces/drives] [outcome].
“Manufacturer-trained service engineers guarantee optimal performance and long-term reliability, reinforcing customer trust.”
“Fully ISO-certified after-sales team offering expert maintenance and technical support for installed equipment.”
positioning
State the differentiated market position.
[Company]'s [attributes] make it [position] for [audience].
“Its independent position and strong local expertise make it a preferred distributor for manufacturers seeking Benelux market access.”
“Fragmented market with Irmaco as a unique one-stop-shop across the full spectrum of dust-explosion and process-fire solutions.”
comparative
Contrast with generalist/competing models.
Unlike [others], [Company] [does X requiring Y].
“Unlike generalist distributors, the company focuses on high-margin niches requiring specialized commercial and technical expertise.”
“Active in specialized medical segments with structurally high margins.”
historic-development
Show the platform built over time.
[Successive/already] [Company] has [transformed/professionalised] [into].
“Management has already driven substantial professionalization across core operations and organizational structure.”
“Successive expansion into adjacent specializations has transformed the business into a diversified platform.”
future-opportunity
Point to headroom / the buyer's upside.
[Opportunities remain / further] [action] offers [route to value].
“Opportunities remain to further optimize warehousing and ERP systems to enhance efficiency and further scalability.”
“Further geographic expansion offers a clear route to replicate the established model in adjacent markets.”
market-trend-plus-relevance
Structural trend -> why it helps the company.
[Trend] favours [companies with the company's attributes].
“Operates in a resilient healthcare sector supported by ageing demographics, rising chronic disease burden and increasing public & private health expenditures across the Benelux.”
“Tighter product regulation favours distributors with established quality systems and local regulatory expertise.”
metric-led
Open with the number that matters.
[Number/share] [provides/represents] [base for a claim].
“High share of recurring revenue, primarily from consumables and service-linked investment goods.”
“More than 450 recurring customers provide a broad base for continued cross-selling.”
two-part-sell-side
Rich highlight bullets; pair a strength with its commercial effect.
[Strength clause], while [company advantage] [supports the payoff].
“BeMedico has built long-term, trust-based exclusive partnerships with internationally recognized OEMs across a range of specialized medical fields.”
“Long-standing and often exclusive supplier partnerships provide access to premium technologies, while local market knowledge supports effective commercialisation.”
compact-label
Scannable KPI captions / highlight chips.
[Adjective] [noun phrase] — no verb.
“High recurring consumables exposure”
“Diversified specialist product portfolio”

Repetition — avoid vs prefer

Avoid
Revenue growth is driven by increased sales.
Margin expansion is driven by improved product mix.
Recurring revenue is driven by the installed base.
Prefer
Revenue growth reflects continued penetration of existing accounts and expansion into adjacent markets.
A favourable shift toward proprietary products supports further margin expansion.
The growing installed base generates recurring maintenance, upgrade and replacement revenue.
